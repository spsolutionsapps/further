ORIGINALS PRODUCTIONS

We create and produce original documentary projects for Amazon Prime Video, National Geographic, History Channel, and other international platforms.

Through The Heritage Guardian, we produce cultural documentaries in collaboration with Ministries of Culture, associations, embassies, and institutions around the world, bringing each country's heritage, traditions, and identity to international audiences.

Visit The Heritage Guardian →

We also created Horses & Humans, the first global documentary platform dedicated to the equestrian world. A documentary series featuring stories from more than 10 countries, distributed through Amazon Prime Video, History Channel, and other international platforms.

ABOUT US

At GAUCHO FILMS, we don't create content just to look good. We create stories designed to connect with each client's audience, understanding what they want to communicate, who they want to reach, and the impact they want to make.

We combine strategy, storytelling, and a cinematic approach to turn every production into a powerful tool to build identity, create impact, and make brands seen, felt, and remembered.
